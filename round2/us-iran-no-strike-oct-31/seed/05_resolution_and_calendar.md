# How and when the question resolves

- Deadline: 11:59 PM Iran Standard Time on 31 October 2026 (20:29 UTC).
- Under the question's rules (text as of 24 October 2026), the answer becomes NO only if a US-initiated air strike or surface-to-surface missile strike directly hits Iran's land territory or internal waters by that deadline.
- Counted weapons: bombs, air-to-surface missiles, air-launched drones, one-way attack drones, and cruise or ballistic missiles.
- Not counted: Israeli strikes, US strikes on proxies in Iraq or Yemen, hits on Iranian ships at sea or aircraft in the air, intercepted munitions and their debris, naval gunfire, artillery, small arms, ground raids, cyber operations, FPV drones, and threats or announcements.
- Resolution sources are official US and Iranian government and military statements plus a consensus of credible reporting.
- If reports conflict, the question can stay open up to three full calendar days (Iran time) after the first credible report, even past 31 October 2026.
- Precedent: on 9 July 2026 Iranian media reported strikes at Bushehr and Konarak that a US official denied.
- Precedent: on 19 October 2026 Iran's navy reported two "light projectiles" fired at Konarak, a US official denied it, and by 24 October 2026 the weapon type was still unclear.
- Calendar: Israel holds elections on 27 October 2026; President Pezeshkian is due to meet Putin in Moscow in the week of 26 October 2026; US midterms are on 3 November 2026; Washington plans three carriers around Iran by end-November 2026.
