# Background: the 2026 US-Iran war and its pauses

- 28 February 2026: the US and Israel struck Iran and killed its supreme leader; Iran hit Israel, US bases and Gulf states and restricted the Strait of Hormuz.
- 8 April 2026: a Pakistan-mediated two-week US-Iran ceasefire took effect; Trump extended it on 21 April 2026.
- 13 April 2026: a US naval blockade of Iranian ports began.
- 7 May 2026: US strikes hit Hormozgan province, which Washington called self-defence; on 25 May 2026 US strikes hit missile sites and mine-laying boats at Bandar Abbas.
- 17 June 2026: the US and Iranian presidents signed the Islamabad Memorandum with a 60-day window for final terms.
- 7-8 July 2026: fighting resumed and Trump called the memorandum "over"; the blockade was reinstated on 14 July 2026.
- 24-27 July 2026: the US paused after about two weeks of nightly strikes, then hit dozens of IRGC targets on 29-30 July 2026.
- 30 August 2026: a roughly monthlong lull ended with US strikes on rocket launchers on Larak Island; Iran fired missiles at US-hosting bases on 31 August 2026.
- 1 September 2026: US strikes hit IRGC air defences, radars, naval and mine-laying sites in southern Iran.
- 5 September 2026: US forces hit three Iranian tankers at sea, outside Iran's land territory.
- 20 October 2026: Gulf News, citing IranWire, reported that 1 September 2026 was the last US strike on Iranian territory.
