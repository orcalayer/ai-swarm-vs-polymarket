# Latest polls: Chow's lead narrowed sharply in October 2026

In early September 2026 Chow had a wide lead: Liaison Strategies (IVR, n=1,000, fielded September 4-5, 2026) put her at 50% to Bradford's 39%. Nanos Research for CP24/CTV (phone and online, n=755, fielded September 30 - October 4, 2026) had Chow 52.4%, Bradford 42.6% and Alexander 4% among decided voters. After Alexander quit on October 6, 2026, Nanos (n=757, October 8-10, 2026) had Chow 51% to 47%, and Nanos (n=762, margin 3.6 points, October 15-18, 2026) had Chow 49.8% to 46.6%, with 16% of all respondents undecided. Liaison had Chow 51% to 46% on October 8-9, 2026, but Bradford 49% to Chow 47% on October 18-19, 2026 (n=1,000, margin 3.1 points). Mainstreet Research (IVR, n=1,000, October 16-18, 2026) had Bradford 48.9% to Chow 47.5%. Build Canada's own poll (SMS-to-online, n=991, October 11-16, 2026) had Bradford 48% to Chow 44%. Pollara, commissioned by CUPE Local 416, which endorsed Chow on October 1, 2026, had Chow 53% to 46% (n=1,099, October 13-19, 2026) and 55% to 44% in a release dated October 23, 2026 that counted respondents who had already voted. By region, the October 15-21, 2026 polls put Chow ahead downtown and Bradford ahead in North York and Etobicoke, with Scarborough close (Liaison, October 18-19, 2026: Chow 49%, Bradford 48%).

Sources: CBC (Sep 2026, Liaison), CP24 (6 Oct 2026, 13 Oct 2026, 20 Oct 2026, Nanos), Global News (21 Oct 2026, 23 Oct 2026), NOW Toronto (22 Oct 2026), Build Canada (19 Oct 2026), The Globe and Mail (23 Oct 2026)
