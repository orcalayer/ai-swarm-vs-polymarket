# The case for YES: why Olivia Chow could win on October 26, 2026

Chow is the incumbent, and Mainstreet's Quito Maggi said on October 20, 2026 that she had "led wire to wire" in his firm's polling before the race tightened in October 2026; Liaison's poll of September 4-5, 2026 had her 11 points ahead. Among the six news-reported polls fielded between October 11 and October 21, 2026, three show Chow ahead (by 3 to 11 points) and three show Bradford ahead (by 1 to 4 points). Nanos (October 15-18, 2026) found Chow ahead by 4 points among the most certain voters, while Bradford led by 4 among less certain ones. Pollara's release of October 23, 2026 gave Chow better personal ratings (48% positive versus 36% for Bradford) and leads on affordability, safety, taxes and working with other governments. Her support is strong among women (60% to 39% in Pollara, October 13-19, 2026) and voters aged 18-34 (above 55% in Nanos, October 15-18, 2026). She is backed by CUPE Local 416 (October 1, 2026), and The Globe and Mail on October 23, 2026 listed Kathleen Wynne, NDP leader Marit Stiles, David Suzuki, Feist and Sarah Polley among her endorsers. Premier Doug Ford praised her work on September 16, 2026. Her 2026 tax increase was a modest 2.2% (adopted February 10, 2026), and the 2026 budget froze TTC fares. Bradford's plan to turn Toronto Water into a utility was opposed by the water workers' union and 15 councillors (reported October 21, 2026), and a union-commissioned poll found 60% worried about privatization.
