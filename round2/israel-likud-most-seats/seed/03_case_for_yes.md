# Arguments that Likud finishes first

In the three final TV polls of 23 October 2026, Likud trailed Yashar by only 2 to 3 seats, while the Maariv poll of the same day stated a maximum sampling error of 3%. Two pollsters put Likud clearly ahead: Direct Polls for i24NEWS had Likud 26 to Yashar 20 on 20 October 2026, and Channel 14 had Likud 30 to Yashar 20 on 22 October 2026. Jewish Insider noted on 22 October 2026 that Direct Polls was regarded as the most accurate pollster of the previous election. Mainstream pollsters underestimated Likud last time: their final pre-election polls of 26-28 October 2022 gave it 30 or 31 seats, while Likud won 32 on 1 November 2022. Likud voters look firmer: in a Walla poll published on 20 October 2026, 68% of Likud voters were completely sure of their vote, against 40% of Yashar voters and 23% of Together voters. Some of Yashar's soft support could still drift to its own allies, since Together gained two seats in the Maariv poll of 23 October 2026. Mainstream polls have also shown ties: Likud and Yashar were level at 21 in a Zman Yisrael poll of 30 September-1 October 2026 and at 20 on Channel 13 on 7 October 2026. According to the JC average of 22 October 2026, Likud had recovered from about 17 seats in September 2026 to about 20.

Sources: Haaretz (23 Oct 2026), Jerusalem Post (20 Oct 2026; 23 Oct 2026), The Media Line (23 Oct 2026), Jewish Insider (22 Oct 2026), The JC (22 Oct 2026), Al Jazeera (20 Oct 2026), Wikipedia poll tables (read 24 Oct 2026)
