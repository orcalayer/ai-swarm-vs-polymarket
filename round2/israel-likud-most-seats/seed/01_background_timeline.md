# Background and timeline of the 2026 Knesset race

In the previous general election on 1 November 2022, Likud became the largest party with 32 seats, and it still held 32 seats when the outgoing Knesset voted to dissolve on 17 July 2026. Former army chief Gadi Eisenkot founded his own party, Yashar, on 16 September 2025. Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid merged Bennett 2026 and Yesh Atid into one list, Together (Beyachad), led by Bennett, on 26 April 2026. The coalition fixed the election date on 12 July 2026, and the final dissolution vote of 17 July 2026, with no votes against, set the election for 27 October 2026. Likud held its primary on 17 August 2026; Eli Cohen, Amir Ohana and Yariv Levin were placed directly behind Benjamin Netanyahu. Chili Tropper merged his party into Yashar on 6 September 2026. A total of 38 lists were submitted by the filing deadline of 8 September 2026. Likud filed its own list on 8 September 2026 without a partner party, but the slate also carries New Hope figures such as Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar under a Likud-New Hope merger. Benny Gantz pulled Blue and White out of the race on 19 October 2026, after polls kept showing it below the 3.25% threshold. The 120 seats are elected by national proportional representation, so the largest list is simply the one with the most votes after the threshold.
