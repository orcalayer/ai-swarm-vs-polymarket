# Latest seat polls before the 27 October 2026 vote

The last polls allowed by law were published on 23 October 2026, and no new polls may appear until exit polls at 22:00 on 27 October 2026. On 23 October 2026 all three main TV polls put Yashar first and Likud second: Channel 12 had Yashar 23 and Likud 21, Channel 13 had Yashar 22 and Likud 19, and Kan 11 had Yashar 22 and Likud 20. A Maariv poll by Lazar Research published on 23 October 2026 (fieldwork 19-22 October 2026) had Yashar 21, Likud 19 and Together 15. An Israel Hayom poll by Kantar on 22 October 2026 had Yashar 23 and Likud 21. A Walla poll published on 20 October 2026 had Yashar 23 and Likud 19. Two pollsters showed the reverse: Direct Polls for i24NEWS had Likud 26 and Yashar 20 on 20 October 2026, and Channel 14 had Likud 30 and Yashar 20 on 22 October 2026. Among 41 published polls with fieldwork from 27 September to 22 October 2026, Yashar led 25, Likud led 14 (all from Channel 14 or i24NEWS) and 2 were ties. In the three TV polls of 23 October 2026, Netanyahu's bloc had 50 seats and the opposition bloc 52 to 56, so neither reached the 61-seat majority.

Sources: Haaretz (23 Oct 2026), Times of Israel (23 Oct 2026), Jerusalem Post (20 Oct 2026; 23 Oct 2026), The Media Line (23 Oct 2026), Wikipedia poll table (read 24 Oct 2026)
