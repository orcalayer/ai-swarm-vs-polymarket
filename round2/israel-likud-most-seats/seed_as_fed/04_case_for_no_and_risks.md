# Arguments against Likud finishing first, and risks

All three main TV polls published on 23 October 2026 (Channel 12, Channel 13 and Kan 11) placed Yashar ahead of Likud. Apart from Channel 14 and i24NEWS, no poll with fieldwork between 27 September and 22 October 2026 showed Likud ahead of Yashar; the best Likud result in that group was a tie. Jewish Insider reported on 22 October 2026 that Channel 14's polls are run by Shlomo Filber, a former Netanyahu campaign aide, and called the Channel 14 and i24NEWS polls outliers. Direct Polls itself overshot Likud in 2022, giving it 34 seats in its poll of 27 October 2022 against the 32 won on 1 November 2022. Haaretz reported on 23 October 2026 that 56% of religious Zionists who voted Likud in 2022 do not plan to do so this time, leaning toward Itamar Ben-Gvir's or Bezalel Smotrich's parties. A Channel 12 poll reported on 11 May 2026 found that only 58% of 2022 Likud voters planned to vote Likud again, with 10% heading to Together and 6% to Yashar. The JC linked a widening of the opposition lead on 22 October 2026 to the dispersal of Gantz's voters after Blue and White quit the race on 19 October 2026. In Maariv's poll of 23 October 2026, Eisenkot led Netanyahu 49% to 41% as preferred prime minister. A risk for both sides is that on 23 October 2026 several small lists, including the Reservists and Ofer Winter's party, sat near the 3.25% threshold, so wasted votes could move the big lists by a seat or two.

Sources: Haaretz (23 Oct 2026), Times of Israel (23 Oct 2026), Jewish Insider (22 Oct 2026), JFeed (11 May 2026), The JC (22 Oct 2026), Jerusalem Post (23 Oct 2026), Wikipedia poll tables for 2022 and 2026 (read 24 Oct 2026)
