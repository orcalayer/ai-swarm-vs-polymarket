# How and when the question is decided

Voting on 27 October 2026 runs from 07:00 to 22:00, and the TV exit polls released at 22:00 that night are projections, not results. Soldiers began early voting on 20 October 2026 at about 850 military polling stations, and diplomatic staff abroad voted on 15-16 October 2026; these ballots are counted after the regular count. About 7.3 million Israelis are eligible to vote, according to a Central Elections Committee figure reported on 20 October 2026. Under Article 11 of Basic Law: The Knesset, official results must be published within eight days of election day, which means by 4 November 2026. When the lists were filed by 8 September 2026, Likud registered under the ballot letters Mem-Chet-Lamed as "Likud under the leadership of Benjamin Netanyahu for Prime Minister". Only the seats won by the Likud candidate list itself count; bloc totals, the coalition that later governs, the person asked to form a government and exit polls do not count. A tie in seats is broken by the number of valid votes for each list, and after that by alphabetical order. The outcome is taken from a consensus of credible reporting, and from the Central Elections Committee's official results if there is any doubt. If results are not definitively known by 30 June 2027, Likud is not counted as the winner.

Sources: Times of Israel (16 Oct 2026; 23 Oct 2026), Al Jazeera (20 Oct 2026), Basic Law: The Knesset (Article 11), Ynet (Central Elections Committee ballot letters, Sep 2026), question resolution rules (read 24 Oct 2026)
