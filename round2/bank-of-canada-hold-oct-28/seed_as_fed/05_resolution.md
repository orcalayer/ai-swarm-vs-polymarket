# How and when the October 2026 decision is announced and judged

The Bank of Canada will announce its decision at 9:45 a.m. Eastern Time on Wednesday 28 October 2026, together with the October 2026 Monetary Policy Report. Governor Tiff Macklem and Senior Deputy Governor Carolyn Rogers hold a press conference at about 10:30 a.m. ET on 28 October 2026. The question compares the target for the overnight rate set on 28 October 2026 with its level just before, 2.25% since 29 October 2025.

The outcome is "no change" only if the target stays exactly at 2.25% on 28 October 2026. Any increase or decrease of any size counts as a change: moves under 25 basis points are rounded up to 25, larger moves to the nearest 25 (ties away from zero), so even a 10 basis-point move is not "no change". The Bank sets a single target rate, not a range, so the upper-bound clause in the rules should not matter.

If the 28 October 2026 announcement is postponed but made before the next scheduled decision on 9 December 2026, the postponed decision counts. If it is cancelled or no decision comes before 9 December 2026, the outcome is "no change". An emergency move made outside the 28 October 2026 announcement would not count.

The last major data before the decision were the September jobs survey of 9 October 2026 and September CPI of 19 October 2026. The Bank's summary of deliberations for the 2 September 2026 decision came out on 16 September 2026, so the summary for this decision would likely follow about two weeks after 28 October 2026.
